Fee Rate Advisories

Aug. 13, 2024

The SEC issues fee rate advisories to inform the public of any developments relating to transaction fees or filing fees paid to the Commission.

Transaction fees are determined in accordance with Section 31 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, which requires self-regulatory organizations to pay the Commission twice annually a fee based on the aggregate dollar amount of certain sales of securities. This fee rate may change annually as part of the Commission’s budget appropriation for a given fiscal year. 

Filing fees are determined in accordance with Section 6(b) of the Securities Act of 1933, which requires the Commission to collect fees from issuers on the registration of securities. This fee rate may change annually as part of the Commission’s budget appropriation for a given fiscal year.
